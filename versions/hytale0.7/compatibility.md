# Multiplayer and known limits

The manifest declares **Hytale >=0.7.4 <0.8.0**. Compilation uses the pinned 0.7.4 SDK; focused native single-player checks used **0.7.5**. This does not certify every future 0.7 patch. Mega Bench with companion API1 is required; choose the exact dependency specified by your Disks file.

Storage and rack actions are **personal to their owner**. This Alpha is intended for experimental single-player use and arranged multiplayer testing. Actual two-client ownership, simultaneous actions and craft-time bridge protection remain unverified. Do not entrust valuable shared-world contents to it yet.

Native checks cover generated-ore harvesting, paid starter progression, transfers, filled-disk upgrades, vertical racks, disk movement, disconnect/reconnect, crafting across two racks and normal save/reload. They do not replace your machine's playtest. Human visual/listening review and optional Backpack checks remain open.

Before a stable release, minor updates may change recipes, balance, behaviour or saved data. Back up your whole world and player data before installing or updating. There are no cables, chest import/export, remote access, wireless storage or shared-owner disks in this version.

No player configuration is required. Capacity and the 16-rack limit are fixed in this implementation.
